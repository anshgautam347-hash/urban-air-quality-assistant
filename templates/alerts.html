Standard Public Health Action Matrix

AQI Tier Classification General Public Guidance Sensitive Groups & Children
0 – 50 Good Air quality is satisfactory. Enjoy regular outdoor sports and exercise. No precautions required.
51 – 100 Moderate Air quality is acceptable. Most individuals can go about standard routines. Unusually sensitive people should monitor for throat irritation or wheezing.
101 – 150 Unhealthy for Sensitive General public is unlikely to be affected; keep afternoon windows closed. Children, seniors, and asthma sufferers should reduce prolonged outdoor exertion.
151 – 200 Unhealthy Everyone may experience irritation. Move high-cardio exercises indoors. Wear certified N95 masks for unavoidable commutes. Eliminate outdoor play.
201 – 300 Very Unhealthy Health alert: increased health risk for entire population. Seal windows. Strict indoor stay. Run continuous HEPA purifiers; avoid outdoor transit.
301 – 500 Hazardous Emergency conditions: all outdoor recreation and transit should be suspended. Remain inside purified clean rooms. Medical supplies and inhalers ready.
